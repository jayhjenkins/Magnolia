This is how my HTML pages look. Build to it exactly.

Layout
- One white content card on a lightly tinted page, centered, max ~1060px.
- Small uppercase eyebrow above the title; uppercase section labels in the accent color; sections divided by hairlines.
- One accent color for headline numbers and labels; status shown as colored text.

Components
- Stat row of 3-4 big numbers with short uppercase labels.
- Ruled tables with right-aligned numbers.
- Callouts and left-ruled quote blocks for emphasis.

Never
- No pill, chip, or badge components.
- No helper text that explains what a section shows or restates a label.
- No emoji. No em dashes.
