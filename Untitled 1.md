5. Refine the existing Home Services section (tighten, clarify, improve)

Focus on the non-emergency use cases.

A simple way to think about home services (segmentation)
1. Emergency: mostly after-hours; a voice feature that hooks into our vendor network.
2. Non-emergency, non-recurring: the "intent moment." Suggest a vendor in context (for example, alongside an ARC request). Be helpful, not salesy. Probably about 3 preferred vendors rather than a large marketplace (how many vendors we need is an open question).
3. Non-emergency, recurring: vendor pay that just works, like Uber, with no payment handoff.
4. Digital only: the home insurance partnership for residents is already in progress (Q1, possibly Q2).
5. (Agentic Pillar) Proactive: we collect home data (roof age, water heater age, last exterior paint, foundation type), for example through an insurance quote form hosted on our page. We then make proactive recommendations to help residents maintain their biggest asset.

Resident journey flow
- Trigger/problem → match (liquidity) → recommendation → booking → payment → scheduling/confirmation → follow-up → service rating, which feeds learning and personalization.
- Show how the flow differs by category (for example, recurring skips booking; digital-only skips scheduling).

Vantaca Wallet (new idea for "miscellaneous payments")
- One-click, "charge it to my tab" payments, used for both vendor services and miscellaneous HOA payments (clubhouse deposit, key fob fee).
- Note: the accounting for miscellaneous payments is the tricky part.

Booking and scheduling UX
- The resident provides preferred time blocks (like a candidate interview scheduler). We coordinate with the vendor by SMS or voice agent, or by API where one exists (for example, field-service platforms like ServiceTitan).
- The key resident question to answer: "When are you going to show up?" Show confirmation and "on the way" status.
- Support both directions: showing a vendor's availability, or collecting the resident's availability and then finding vendors who are free.
- Longer term: vendors keep their calendar in Vantaca because it brings them more deals.
- SMS-first coordination covers most vendors, since they run their businesses by text.
